Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BERU AI ("BERU", "we", "us") handles information when you use our application.

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive personal information in your chats.

1. Information we process

  • Google account information: when you sign in with Google, we receive information made available by Google Sign-In, such as your name, email address, profile image, and account identifier, to authenticate you and maintain your BERU account.
  • Chats and attachments: text you submit and images you choose to attach are sent to BERU's AI service so it can generate a response.
  • Support reports: information and messages you submit through the in-app report form are sent to our service for support and troubleshooting.
  • Technical and usage information: our services may process basic request, security, and operational data needed to provide, protect, and maintain the application.

2. Where information is stored

Your sign-in session token and a profile cache are stored in your browser's local storage. Your conversation history is currently saved in local storage on the device and browser you use, rather than synchronized as a cloud chat history by the application. Clearing browser storage or changing devices may remove or make that history unavailable. Image data is not included in the saved chat-history record.

Information sent to BERU's authentication and AI endpoints is processed by the services and infrastructure used to operate BERU, including Cloudflare. The service may also rely on Google to provide sign-in. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

3. How we use information

  • To sign you in, create and secure your account, and maintain your session.
  • To answer prompts and process images you intentionally submit.
  • To receive and respond to support reports, investigate errors, and improve reliability.
  • To prevent abuse, protect the service, and administer available account features.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate BERU, provide Google authentication, process AI requests, maintain security, or comply with applicable law. We do not control the independent privacy practices of those providers.

5. Retention and your choices

Local chat history remains in your browser until you delete it or clear that browser's site data. Authentication and operational information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to operate and secure the service, resolve reports, and meet legal obligations. Contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion requests. We will assess and respond to requests as required by applicable law.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to a developing online service. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

BERU AI is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as BERU develops. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
